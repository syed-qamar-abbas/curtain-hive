## Sourcing and delivery

The new photographs were found through search and their original Pexels pages, then reviewed visually for relevance. The [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) allows free use and modification; source and photographer credits appear in the lightbox and detail view. The gallery is design inspiration, and does not claim these independent photographs depict Curtain Hive installations. No premium images, watermarked previews, new AI images or known 3D renders are included. A render candidate was rejected during review.

All 25 originals are saved locally as WebP at up to 1600px on the longest side, quality 85. Responsive thumbnails are 800px wide at quality 82. Total new full images: 3.95 MiB; thumbnails: 1.18 MiB. Dimensions, checksums, download/source URLs, license and review date are recorded in `research/inspiration-images.json`. The page never depends on a remote hotlink.

The approved staggered two-column gallery, rounded image cards, burgundy selection and existing typography are retained. All view initially presents 12 ideas; Explore more rooms reveals 12 more, then the remaining nine. Each category shows its complete six- or seven-image collection. The category is preserved in the URL for sharing, reload and browser history. The lightbox navigates across the entire selected category, with previous/next keys, Escape, focus return and the matching service consultation link.

A shared gallery record now drives the grid, 33 detail routes and SEO metadata. All 25 new pages receive their own title, description, canonical, relevant social image and breadcrumbs. The build produces 90 complete HTML routes. The existing client-preview indexing policy is retained.
